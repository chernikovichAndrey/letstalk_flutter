Privacy Policy — Messenger

Last updated: February 2, 2026

Messenger respects users' right to privacy and ensures the protection of personal data in accordance with the requirements of the App Store, Google Play, and applicable laws.

1. Data We Collect

When using Messenger, we may collect the following data:

  • data provided by the user during registration (phone number);
  • data required for messaging, audio, and video call functionality;
  • technical data (device type, operating system version, application language);
  • service data (connection status).
❗ Messenger does not use user data for advertising purposes and does not sell personal data to third parties.

2. Messages and Content

  • Messenger is intended for exchanging messages, making audio and video calls, and sharing media files and documents.
  • The content of personal messages is not analyzed and is not used for marketing purposes.
  • Messenger is not a party to user communications.

3. How We Use Data

Collected data is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • ensuring proper operation of the application;
  • providing messaging, calling, and file-sharing features;
  • ensuring security and preventing misuse;
  • providing technical support to users;
  • improving the stability and quality of the application.

4. Data Sharing with Third Parties

We do not share users' personal data with third parties, except in the following cases:

  • as required by law;
  • in response to official requests from authorized governmental or law enforcement authorities;
  • to protect the rights and security of users and the application.

5. Data Security

Messenger applies modern technical and organizational measures to protect data, including:

  • secure data transmission channels (SSL/TLS);
  • restricted access to data;
  • measures to prevent unauthorized access.

6. Data Storage and Deletion

  • Data is stored only for as long as necessary for the operation of the service.
  • Users may stop using Messenger at any time.
  • Deleting an account results in the removal of associated data within a reasonable period, unless otherwise required by law.

7. Use of Cookies and Similar Technologies

Messenger may use cookies and similar technologies exclusively for:

  • ensuring stable operation of the application;
  • saving user preferences;
  • analyzing technical errors and improving the service.
  • Cookies are not used for advertising tracking.

8. User Rights

Users have the right to:

  • receive information about their personal data;
  • request correction or deletion of their data;
  • stop using the service at any time.

9. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is always available in the Messenger application and on the official Messenger website.
